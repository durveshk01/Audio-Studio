Audio Studio needs a current Android WebView

Update Android System WebView and Google Chrome through your device's app store, then close and reopen Audio Studio. WebView version 99 or newer is required for local audio workers and metadata editing.

Once WebView is updated, Audio Studio's bundled workspaces and audio processing work offline. The APK does not need an Internet permission.

If WebView is already current, reinstall the latest Audio Studio APK to restore its bundled assets. Save any important exports to Downloads before uninstalling.
